BIMUniXchange · Open-source

From BIM models to semantic knowledge graphs

One workbench for IFC models (IFC2x3, IFC4, IFC4x3, .ifczip) and BIMUniXchange CSV exports from Revit and ArchiCAD. Every relationship becomes a typed edge aligned to ifcOWL, BOT and Brick, enriched with inferred links, and ready for network analysis, quality auditing and export to RDF, GraphML or Neo4j.
