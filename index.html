Capabilities

Built for research rigour and day-to-day model QA

From a robust STEP parser to network science and open exports, every step is transparent: edges keep their source relationship, and inferred facts state the rule that produced them.

Fast, faithful IFC parsing

Streaming ISO 10303-21 indexer with lazy decoding. Geometry is never materialised — a 90 MB file indexes in about a second.

Semantic enrichment

Inferred hosting, port-to-port feeds, space adjacency and door / stair access, storey location, type-inherited properties.

Ontology alignment

Classes and relations mapped to ifcOWL, BOT and Brick; properties exported as LBD PROPS. Coverage is reported per class.

Network science

Degree, PageRank, betweenness, closeness, clustering, Louvain communities, modularity, diameter and shortest paths.

Space syntax

Access graphs from space boundaries with mean depth, relative asymmetry and integration — rooted at the exterior.

Model health

20 checks — GlobalIds, containment, typing, materials, Pset_*Common, classification, MEP topology — scored and explorable.

Query & catalogue

Property query builder with wildcards and numeric operators, a property catalogue with coverage statistics, colour by any property.

Open exports

RDF Turtle, Neo4j Cypher, GraphML, JSON and CSV with metrics, publication-ready SVG, plus health and mapping reports.

How it works

From file to knowledge graph in five steps

01

Load

Drop an .ifc, .ifczip or CSV. Nothing leaves the browser.

02

Index

Every STEP instance is indexed by byte range; arguments decode on demand.

03

Model

Objectified relationships become typed edges; psets, units and materials resolve.

04

Infer

Rules add hosting, flow, adjacency and discipline — each with provenance.

05

Analyse & export

Lenses, metrics, queries and audits — then RDF, GraphML or Neo4j.

Standards & formats

Speaks the languages of linked data and graph tooling

Take the graph wherever your work continues — triple stores, graph databases, network-analysis software or a paper figure.

Ontologies

ifcOWLEvery class typed against the buildingSMART IFC4 ADD2 TC1 OWL ontology.
BOTSite, Building, Storey, Space, Element; hasStorey, containsElement, hostsElement, adjacentZone.
BrickEquipment classes (pumps, fans, valves, sensors…), feeds and isPartOf for systems.
LBD PROPSLevel-1 properties as literals for straightforward SPARQL.

Exports

RDF TurtleWhole model or visible subgraph, ready for GraphDB, Jena or rdflib.
Neo4j CypherLabelled property graph with typed relationships and an inferred flag.
GraphML · CSVGephi, yEd, Cytoscape and NetworkX, including centralities and positions.
SVG · PNG · JSONVector figures for publications and node-link JSON with properties.
Who it's for

Research questions and project deliverables

Research

  • Building network science — centrality and community structure of spatial, MEP and structural graphs.
  • Space syntax from BIM — integration and depth straight from IFC space boundaries.
  • Linked building data — BOT / Brick-aligned RDF for semantic web and digital-twin studies.
  • Information requirements — property coverage across classes and authoring tools.

Industry

  • Model QA / QC — a scored health report before hand-over, with every finding traceable to objects.
  • MEP tracing — follow flow from plant to terminal and find disconnected ports.
  • Digital-twin onboarding — export Brick / BOT graphs or a Neo4j database for FM platforms.
  • Cross-tool coordination — compare Revit and ArchiCAD exports against IFC classes.
BIM → XR pipeline

From authoring tools to immersive construction review

BIMUniXchange started as an interoperability pipeline: export geometry and complete metadata from Revit or ArchiCAD, and bring both into Unity for XR — Quest 3, mobile and desktop VR.
