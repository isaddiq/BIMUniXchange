How it works

From raw BIM data to an interactive network graph in four steps.

1

Export your model

Export your BIM model as a CSV from Revit or ArchiCAD, or save as an IFC file from any compliant BIM application.

2

Choose the right tool

Select the analyzer that matches your export format — Revit CSV, ArchiCAD CSV, or universal IFC.

3

Upload your file

Drag and drop or browse for your file. All processing runs in your browser — no data is ever sent to a server.

4

Explore the graph

Pan, zoom, and filter in 2D — or switch to immersive 3D view. Export as PNG, SVG, or JSON when you're done.
